THE MODULAR LAPTOP

Have you ever felt the need to buy a new laptop just because you are stuck on a particular storage or RAM.Have you ever thought of a laptop that it's storage can be upgraded to your choice.Well,believe it or not what if I told you that there is a laptop that it's storage and RAM can be upgraded.This laptop is called the "Framework laptop". Allow me to tell you more about this laptop.

Framework laptops are modular repairable laptop you can upgrade and fix yourself. This feature gives it an edge over other laptops.And this makes it a suitable laptop for programmers because what if you run out of storage or need more ROM for processing. The designers of this amazing laptop are Nirav Patel,Poyu Chen and Kieran Levin. The current models of this laptop are Framework 12,13 and 16.For more information about their laptops visit their original site here.

One of the feature I like most about this laptop is the opportunity to expand its memory. This is a very useful feature because most laptop don't give this feature.Also the RAM is upgradable,giving you an opportunity to improve the speed of your laptop.

If you are planning buy any laptop I will advise you to checkout the framework laptop on their origanal site here.Trust me this is a laptop you can rely on for years.I wish to own one because of how amazing it is.

WHY THIS LAPTOP IS SPECIAL

  • Upgradable:You can upgrade storage or ROM when needed
  • Repairable:Every part can be replaced like battery,screen or keyboard because they are independent parts.
  • CustomisableYou can choose your own ports like USB-C,HDMI,USB-A etc.
